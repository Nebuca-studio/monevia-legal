# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: May 10, 2026**
**Version: 1.1**

This policy describes how the **Monevia** application ("the App") processes personal data of users ("you"). It complies with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR") and the French law n° 78-17 of January 6, 1978, as amended (*Loi Informatique et Libertés* — "French Data Protection Act").

By installing and using the App, you acknowledge that you have read this policy.

---

## 1. Identity of the data controller

The data controller within the meaning of Article 4 of the GDPR is **Nebuca**.

- **Contact email**: nebuca.contact@gmail.com

The full identity of the controller, postal address, SIREN registration number and all other legal information are available in the **[Legal Notice](legal-notice-en.md)** of the App.

No Data Protection Officer (DPO) has been appointed, as such designation is not required given the nature and volume of processing activities (Article 37 GDPR).

---

## 2. Guiding principle: exclusively local data storage

**All data you enter into the App is stored exclusively on your device.** No data is transmitted to Nebuca or to any third party at any time. The App operates entirely offline, with no remote server, no user account, no authentication, and no tracking.

The only exceptions to this principle are:

1. **In-app purchase transactions**, handled exclusively by Apple Inc. (iOS) or Google LLC (Android), which act as the merchant of record for premium features. Nebuca receives no banking data; we receive only, from the platform, the information indicating that a license has been activated for your platform identifier.
2. **Automatic device backups** (iCloud, Google Drive Auto-Backup), performed by your operating system independently of Monevia. These backups may include the App's content. They are governed by your contractual relationship with Apple or Google, not by Nebuca. You can disable them in your device settings.

## 3. Categories of data processed

The following data may be entered by you and stored on your device:

### 3.1 Identification data (optional)
- First name

### 3.2 Personal and tax profile data
- Date of birth (optional)
- Family situation (single, in a relationship, civil union, married, divorced, widowed)
- Number of dependents
- Number of tax shares (*parts fiscales*)
- Professional status (employee, civil servant, self-employed, student, retired, jobseeker, other)
- Type of housing (tenant, owner with or without mortgage, housed free of charge, other)
- Estimated marginal tax bracket (optional)
- Reference taxable income (*revenu fiscal de référence*) (optional)
- Information about a partner, if any: monthly income, marginal tax bracket (all optional)
- Indication of any access to an employee savings scheme (PEE, PERCO)

### 3.3 Declared financial data
- Income (category, amount, frequency)
- Fixed and variable expenses (category, label, amount, frequency, essential/non-essential indicator, loan installment indicator)
- Savings and investment assets (envelope type, balance, opening date if applicable)
- Real estate assets (estimated value, outstanding capital, status as primary residence or rental property)
- Debts (type, outstanding capital, monthly payment, interest rate)
- Financial goals (label, time horizon, target amount, deadline)
- Risk profile (self-assessed level)

### 3.4 Simulated savings plans
- Plan name, description
- Investment vehicles attached to the plan
- Investment lines (name, product type, initial amount, contribution mode and amount, estimated rate of return, fees)

### 3.5 Usage preferences
- Interface language
- Theme (light, dark, automatic)
- List of observations and suggestions you have marked as "addressed"
- Answers to the budget definition questionnaire

No sensitive data within the meaning of Article 9 of the GDPR (racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious or philosophical beliefs, trade union membership, genetic data, biometric data, data concerning health, sex life or sexual orientation) is processed.

No location data, advertising identifier, contacts, photos, microphone or sensor data is collected.

---

## 4. Processing purposes

Data is processed exclusively for the following purposes:

1. **Calculate a personal budget** and present a diagnostic (disposable income, savings rate, debt ratio, emergency fund, needs/wants/savings allocation).
2. **Produce financial projections** on the savings plans you enter (compound interest, fees, contributions).
3. **Generate educational recommendations** tailored to your profile, for indicative purposes only.
4. **Allow comparison** between several savings plans (premium feature).
5. **Allow PDF export** of a plan or of the complete analysis (premium feature).
6. **Remember your interface preferences** (language, theme).

None of the processed data is used for commercial prospecting, targeted advertising, commercial profiling, resale, or sharing with third parties.

## 5. Legal bases

Processing relies, depending on the case, on:

- The **performance of a contract** within the meaning of Article 6.1.b of the GDPR, for the provision of the budget and financial simulation service you request by using the App;
- **Legitimate interest** within the meaning of Article 6.1.f of the GDPR, for the local storage of your interface preferences;
- The **performance of legal obligations** within the meaning of Article 6.1.c of the GDPR, for the retention of records relating to in-app purchase transactions in accordance with applicable accounting legislation.

No processing relies on consent within the meaning of Article 6.1.a, as no data leaves your device.

## 6. Storage and security

### 6.1 Location
Data is stored:
- In a local database (SQLite) located in the App's private storage directory on your device;
- In the operating system's local preferences (NSUserDefaults on iOS, SharedPreferences on Android), associated with the App identifier.

These locations are, by default, isolated from other apps on your device by your operating system's sandbox mechanism.

### 6.2 System backups
As stated in Section 2, the App's content may be included in the automatic backups you have enabled on your device (iCloud Backup for iOS, Auto-Backup for Android). These backups:
- Are encrypted by your operating system and associated with your iCloud or Google account;
- Are outside Nebuca's control;
- Allow restoration of your data when you change devices.

You may at any time disable or exclude the App from system backups in your device settings.

### 6.3 Technical measures
The App implements security measures that are reasonable given the state of the art and the nature of the data:
- Sandbox storage on the device;
- No network exposure;
- Regular updates published through official app stores.

As no security measure can guarantee absolute protection, you are encouraged to protect access to your device with a passcode, pattern, or biometric authentication.

## 9. Retention period

Your data is stored on your device **until you delete it yourself** through one of the following actions:
- Individual deletion of an item (plan, investment line, budget profile…) from the App interface;
- Reset of the budget analysis from the dedicated screen;
- Complete data erasure through the "Erase all my data" feature available in the App settings;
- Uninstalling the App.

No data is retained by Nebuca after deletion by you, as Nebuca holds no copy.

For data relating to in-app purchase transactions, Nebuca retains the strictly necessary accounting evidence (transaction identifier, amount, date) for **ten (10) years** in accordance with Article L.123-22 of the French Commercial Code (*Code de commerce*). This retention covers exclusively the accounting items transmitted by Apple or Google, excluding any data entered into the App.

## 10. Your rights

In accordance with Articles 15 to 22 of the GDPR, you have the following rights:

- **Right of access**: obtain confirmation that data concerning you is being processed and obtain a copy of it;
- **Right to rectification**: correct inaccurate data concerning you;
- **Right to erasure** ("right to be forgotten"): obtain the deletion of your data;
- **Right to restriction** of processing;
- **Right to data portability**: receive your data in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format;
- **Right to object** to processing;
- **Right to provide directives** regarding the fate of your data after your death, in accordance with Article 85 of the French Data Protection Act.

### 10.1 Exercise modalities
Almost all of these rights can be exercised **directly by you within the App**:
- **Access**: every screen allows reading of the data entered;
- **Rectification**: every field is editable;
- **Erasure**: deletion item by item or through reset functions;
- **Portability**: the App natively offers **complete export** of your data in **JSON** and **CSV** formats, as well as a **JSON import** feature allowing you to restore or transfer your data to another device. These features are free and independent of premium status.

For any additional request, you may contact Nebuca at **nebuca.contact@gmail.com**, specifying the subject of your request. Nebuca undertakes to respond within one month from receipt of the request, in accordance with Article 12 of the GDPR.

### 10.2 Identity verification
Given the App's exclusively local architecture, Nebuca has no means of verifying the identity of a requester. In case of reasonable doubt about the identity of the requester, Nebuca may request additional information necessary for such verification, in accordance with Article 12.6 of the GDPR.
